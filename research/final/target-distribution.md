# 2. Target Variable & Archetype Distribution

## Subjective-Objective Capability Matrix

The target variable operationalizes the Subjective-Objective Financial Knowledge Matrix, combining 5 objective quiz items (`M6`–`M10`) and 1 subjective self-assessment rating (`M4`):

- **Objective Score ($S_{\text{obj}}$)**: Unweighted sum of correct responses across `M6` ($=1$), `M7` ($=3$), `M8` ($=2$), `M9` ($=1$), and `M10` ($=2$). Range: $0$ to $5$.
  - $\text{LOW Objective} \iff S_{\text{obj}} \in \{0, 1, 2\}$
  - $\text{HIGH Objective} \iff S_{\text{obj}} \in \{3, 4, 5\}$
- **Subjective Score ($S_{\text{subj}}$)**: Self-assessed knowledge rating `M4`. Range: $1$ to $7$.
  - $\text{LOW Subjective} \iff M4 \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$
  - $\text{HIGH Subjective} \iff M4 \in \{5, 6, 7\}$
